# D&D: The Tyrant's Eye

*Stern, 2025 · color LCD · Premium*

## About the game
Designed by Brian Eddy, who made *Medieval Madness*. Code by Dwight Sullivan. Made with Wizards of the Coast for D&D's 50th anniversary. The rules run deep, and it plays like a real D&D campaign. You can even save your progress with a Stern Insider account. Over the top sits **Rath**, a huge red dragon who moves and blocks your shots.

## The story
You join the Dragonshield Guild in the Forgotten Realms. The dragon queen **Tiamat** is rising, and her generals are waging the War of Dragons. Travel from town to town, finish quests and stop her.

## How to play
- At the start, **pick a hero** with the flipper buttons. Each one is a different class.
- Shoot the yellow **Choose Action** shot to start battles, quests and travel.
- Spell **D-R-A-G-O-N** on the targets under Rath to start **Dragon Multiball**.
- Lock balls on the **Gelatinous Cube** for another multiball.

## Tips
- **#1:** Use the **shield**. Raise it with the action button to save a ball draining down the middle.
- **Ardor** the Paladin hits dragons harder. **Anya** the Rogue finds more treasure.
- **Frey** the Cleric is best against undead. **Helenir** the Wizard gets a longer shield.
- Finished quests can't be replayed, so try something new each game.

## Fun facts
- The voice cast includes Matthew Mercer and Laura Bailey of *Critical Role* and Michael Dorn as Rath.
- On the Premium, Rath's head moves in two directions and fires balls during Dragon Multiball.
