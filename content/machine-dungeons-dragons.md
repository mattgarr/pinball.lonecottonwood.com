# D&D: The Tyrant's Eye

*Stern, 2025 · color LCD*

## The story
A full D&D quest to stop the dragon queen **Tiamat**. Dragons, a beholder, a gelatinous cube and 40+ more monsters.

## How to play
At the start, **pick a class** with the flipper buttons. Then play **modes** (mini-quests) and build to **Dragon Multiball**.

## Tips
- **#1:** pick the **Paladin**. Easiest path to multiball.
- **Rogue** = more treasure. **Cleric** = more damage. **Wizard** = longer shield.
- One mode + one multiball = a good score.

## Fun fact
By Brian Eddy (*Medieval Madness*). Made for D&D's 50th.
