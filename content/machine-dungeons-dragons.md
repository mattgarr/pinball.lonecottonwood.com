# Dungeons & Dragons: The Tyrant's Eye

*Stern, 2025 · full-color LCD display*

## What it is
The newest machine here by 40-plus years — a full-blown fantasy campaign set in D&D's Forgotten Realms, with a voice cast that includes **Matthew Mercer, Laura Bailey, and Michael Dorn**. You're a member of the Dragonshield Guild out to stop the dragon queen **Tiamat**, battling a red dragon, a gelatinous cube, a beholder, and 40-some other monsters across a branching adventure.

## How to play
At the start of each game you **pick a character class** using the flipper buttons — this genuinely changes your strategy. Then you work through **modes** (mini-quests at different dungeon locations), build toward **Dragon Multiball**, and make choices that branch toward different endings. Cascading ramps and loops keep the ball flowing fast.

## Scoring tips
- **If you only do one thing:** at the start, pick the **Paladin** — it makes Dragon Multiball easiest to reach, and multiball is your points.
- Other classes if you're feeling it: **Rogue** (richer treasure), **Cleric** (more damage), **Wizard** (longer shield).
- Just start a mode and start a multiball in one game and you'll already be over a "good" score for most casual players.

## Good to know
Designed by **Brian Eddy** — the legend behind *Attack from Mars* and *Medieval Madness* — making your collection a quiet bookend of his career, from the early-'80s era right up to his 2025 work. Released for D&D's 50th anniversary, with only **740** of the top-tier Limited Editions made.
