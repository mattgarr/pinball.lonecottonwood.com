# Cactus Canyon

*Chicago Gaming, 2021 remake of Bally's 1998 original · Limited Edition*

## About the game
A limited edition remake of Bally's 1998 classic, built new by Chicago Gaming. Only 1,250 Limited Editions were made. The original was designed by Tom Kopera and Matt Coriale, with art by John Youssi. It flows well and is full of cowboy humor.

## The story
Wild West. You're the new sheriff. Beat the **Bart brothers**, save **Polly Peril** from the train and raid the gold mine.

## How to play
The goal is to light all 5 points of your **Lawman Badge**. That starts the final showdown, **High Noon**.
- Shoot the **bad guy** targets when they pop up. Win 4 **Quick Draws** to start **Showdown** multiball.
- Lock 3 balls in the **Gold Mine** for **Gold Mine Multiball**.
- Shoot the **Bart** toy at the back to fight each brother.
- Rescue Polly: hit the left and right ramps fast, before the train reaches her.

## Tips
- **#1:** Hit the bad guys whenever they pop up. Showdown is the easiest multiball.
- In a **Gunfight**, the ball stops and you get a few seconds. Aim, then flip.
- Making one big shot lights the others for a **combo**. Chain them.
- Light both top lanes to raise your **bonus multiplier**.

## Fun facts
- The 1998 original was the last classic Bally/Williams pin. Only 903 were made, with unfinished code.
- This one runs the **Lyman Sheats Complete Upgrade**. Sheats, a pinball legend, helped finish the remake's code. He died in 2022, and his team completed his upgrade. It adds 10 new modes, a saloon toy and a fuller **High Noon**.
