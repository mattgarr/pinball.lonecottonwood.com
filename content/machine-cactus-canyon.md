# Cactus Canyon

*Bally, 1998 · dot-matrix*

## The story
Wild West. You're the new sheriff: beat outlaws, **rescue Polly Peril** from the train, raid the gold mine.

## How to play
Shoot the **bad guy** targets when they pop up. Start the multiballs: **Showdown**, **Stampede**, **Gold Mine**. Each fills your **Sheriff's Badge**. Fill it for the finale, **High Noon**.

## Tips
- **#1:** hit the bad guys whenever they pop up.
- **Showdown** is the easiest multiball.
- Gunfights are quick. Shoot fast.

## Fun fact
The last classic Bally/Williams pin ever. Only **903** made.
