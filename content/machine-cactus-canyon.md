# Cactus Canyon

*Bally, 1998 · dot-matrix display*

## What it is
A Wild West shootout. You're the new sheriff of Cactus Canyon: face down outlaws, win gunfights, **rescue Polly Peril** from the train tracks, raid the gold mine, and survive a stampede. A moving train and a taunting Bart figure live on the playfield. Charming, funny, and rare.

## How to play
Knock down the four "**bad guy**" targets that pop up, and trigger the three multiballs — **Showdown** (shoot the four bad guys), **Stampede** (complete all the ramps and loops), and **Gold Mine**. Doing each of these stamps part of your **Sheriff's Badge**. Complete the whole badge and you unlock the showdown finale, **High Noon at the OK Corral**.

## Scoring tips
- **If you only do one thing:** shoot the bad guy targets whenever they pop up — that's the core of everything.
- Start any multiball you can; **Showdown** is the most beginner-friendly.
- **Quick gunfights** trigger from the inlane — when a bad guy pops up, take the shot fast; they don't wait.

## Good to know
Designed by Tom Kopera and Matt Coriale. This was the very last traditional pinball Bally/Williams ever built before the company pivoted (and soon folded), so it shipped with famously unfinished code — only **903** were made, which is why it's a collector's grail. A modern remake with completed rules exists, so yours may play a little differently than the original.
