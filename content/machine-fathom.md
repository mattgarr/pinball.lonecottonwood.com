# Fathom

*Bally, 1981 · LED scoring*

## About the game
A Bally classic from the golden age of solid-state pinball. Its deep blue undersea artwork is some of the most loved of the early 80s. The playfield is packed with shots, plus an extra flipper up top. It plays fast and rewards clean aim.

## The story
A trip to the bottom of the sea. Mermaids, sea serpents and other monsters of the deep, all in rich blues and greens.

## How to play
Multiball is the heart of the game.
- **Lock a ball** in one of the 2 **saucers** (holes that grab the ball). A row of 3 drop targets pops up in front of it.
- Knock down all 3 to free the ball. That starts 2-ball multiball.
- Free the other saucer during multiball for 3 balls. Scoring doubles with 2 balls and triples with 3.
- Hit the standup target on the right to light the **spinner**. Then rip it for big points.

## Tips
- **#1:** Lock a ball, then knock down the 3 targets in front of it. Multiball multiplies every point.
- During multiball, don't shoot balls back into the saucers. It cuts your multiplier.
- The drop targets also raise your **bonus multiplier**, up to 5x.
- Watch the bottom corners. The lane nearest the wall feeds your flipper. The inner one drains.

## Fun facts
- The lanes by the flippers are flipped from the usual layout. It catches almost everyone.
- Fans love it enough that Haggis Pinball announced a modern remake, **Fathom Revisited**.
