# Centaur

*Bally, 1981 · solid-state, segmented LED scoring*

## What it is
A gloriously menacing fantasy machine — half-man, half-motorcycle creature — wrapped in stark black, white, and red comic-book art. The machine taunts you in a growling, echoing voice and dares you to beat it. One of the all-time cult classics, and brutally fast for its age.

## How to play
The heart of the game is the **O-R-B-S** drop targets in the center. Knock them down in order (O, then R, then B, then S) and you trigger multiball — and Centaur lets you keep adding balls, so a good run snowballs into chaos. There's also a row of 1-2-3-4 targets and a captive ball off to the left to hammer for points.

## Scoring tips
- **If you only do one thing:** shoot the O-R-B-S targets in sequence to start multiball — that's where the points live.
- Complete the **1-2-3-4 targets** in order for big bonus jumps.
- Build your **bonus multiplier** with the lanes across the top (the flipper buttons shift those lights).

## Good to know
Designed by Jim Patla with iconic art by Paul Faris. Centaur had a 5-ball multiball in 1981 — wildly ahead of its time — and that snarling "I am Centaur" speech is one of the most recognizable sounds in pinball.
