# Centaur

*Bally, 1981 · LED scoring*

## The story
A half-man, half-motorcycle beast in black, white and red. It growls and taunts you. A fast cult classic.

## How to play
Hit the **O-R-B-S** drop targets in order to start multiball. Keep adding balls for chaos. Also hammer the **1-2-3-4** targets and the captive ball on the left.

## Tips
- **#1:** O, R, B, S in order. Multiball = points.
- **1-2-3-4** in order = big bonus.
- Top lanes build your **bonus multiplier**. Flippers shift the lights.

## Fun fact
A 5-ball multiball in 1981. Wildly ahead of its time.
