# Centaur

*Bally, 1981 · LED scoring*

## About the game
Designed by Jim Patla, with art by Paul Faris. It was the last game Patla designed on his own. Its black and white playfield still looks like nothing else. It plays fast and loud, and it has a real cult following.

## The story
A centaur, half man and half horse, rides a motorbike through a dark fantasy world. Your job is to beat him. He taunts you when you miss.

## How to play
The big prize is multiball, up to **5 balls**. There's no real lock. The game banks balls, then sets them all loose at once.
- **O-R-B-S** drop targets (below the bumpers): drop all 4 to bank a ball. Drop them in order and a ball joins play right away.
- **Release**: shoot the captive ball in the top left **Queen's Chamber**, behind 4 drop targets. Or hit **1-2-3-4** in order, then the lit target at the upper right. Every banked ball comes out.

## Tips
- **#1:** Hit O, R, B, S in order. It's the quickest way to get more balls on the table.
- In multiball, just keep the balls alive. Nearly every shot scores more.
- The **1-2-3-4** targets pay more each time you stay in order.
- Use the flippers to shift the lit top lanes. Fill all 3 to raise your **bonus multiplier**, up to 5x.

## Fun facts
- With nothing going on, it launches balls just to show off its multiball.
- Bally liked it so much it put out the same game again in 1983 as **Centaur II**.
