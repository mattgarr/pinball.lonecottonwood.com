# Whirlwind

*Williams, 1990 · alphanumeric · has a fan!*

## The story
Storm chasing in Kansas. A real fan **blows wind at you**. Spinning discs fling the ball around.

## How to play
Light the locks, **lock 2 balls**, then start **The Power** multiball. Visit the **cellar** for a random prize. A 3rd flipper on the right hits the left ramp.

## Tips
- **#1:** lock 2 balls for multiball.
- Don't fight the spinning discs. Keep flipping.
- Grab the **cellar** whenever it's lit. Free points.

## Fun fact
By Pat Lawlor (*The Addams Family*). One of the first with a **wizard mode**.
