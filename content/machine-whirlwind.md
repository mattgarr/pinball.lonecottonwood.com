# Whirlwind

*Williams, 1990 · alphanumeric · has a fan!*

## About the game
Designed by Pat Lawlor, who later made *The Addams Family*. Art by John Youssi. It's a follow-up to his earthquake game, *Earthshaker!* It plays fast and wild, with 3 flippers, 6 pop bumpers and spinning discs. Players love it for the chaos.

## The story
Storm chasing on the Kansas plains. You hunt the twister across the map. When the storm hits, a fan on top **blows wind in your face**.

## How to play
- Hit the **compass** targets (NE, NW, SE, SW) to move the storm and light the **lock**.
- **Lock 2 balls**, then start **Feel the Power** multiball.
- In multiball, use the small upper right flipper to hit the ramp for the **Million Plus** jackpot.
- Shoot the **Super Cellar** (a hole on the left) when it's lit. Each visit gives one of 7 prizes, like a lit extra ball.

## Tips
- **#1:** Light and lock 2 balls. Multiball is where the big points are.
- The discs spin when a lock is lit or during multiball. Don't fight them. Wait for the ball to come out.
- The right inlane briefly lights the **Super Cellar**. Go for it right away.
- Learn the upper flipper. It feeds the best shots in the game.

## Fun facts
- It was the first pinball machine with a fan topper. The slogan was "Feel the power of the wind!"
- It was also the first game with a **Grand Champion** high score that survives the usual score resets.
