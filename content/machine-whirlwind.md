# Whirlwind

*Williams, 1990 · alphanumeric scoring · with a fan that blows in your face*

## What it is
Storm-chasing in Kansas. You're trying to wrangle a rampaging tornado, and the machine commits to the bit: there's an actual fan on the back that **blows wind at you** during big moments, plus three spinning discs set into the playfield that fling the ball in random directions. Pure chaotic fun.

## How to play
Hit the targets that direct the storm to light your ball locks, lock two balls, then unleash **"The Power"** — the multiball. Along the way you pay off **Skyway Tolls**, crank up the atmospheric pressure, and visit the **cellar** for a random surprise award. A third flipper up on the right reaches the trickier left ramp.

## Scoring tips
- **If you only do one thing:** lock two balls to start The Power multiball.
- Ride the spinning discs — fighting them is pointless; just keep flipping and the ball usually pops back into play.
- Grab the **cellar award** whenever it's lit; it's free value.

## Good to know
Pat Lawlor's design (he made the best-selling pinball of all time, *The Addams Family*, a couple years later). Whirlwind was one of the very first games with a **"wizard mode"** — a hidden final challenge for great players — a concept nearly every machine copied afterward. Fun touch: all three discs run off a single hidden motor.
