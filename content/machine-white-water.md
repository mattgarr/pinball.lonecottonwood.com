# Whitewater

*Williams, 1993 · dot-matrix*

## About the game
Designed by Dennis Nordman, with art by John Youssi. It came out in the golden age of 90s pinball. The ramps are long and twisty, and a small upper playfield sits at the back. Shots are tough, but making them feels great. It plays fast and a little rough, just like the river.

## The story
A white-water rafting trip. Run the rapids, dodge boulders, go over falls and sneak past **Bigfoot**. The goal is to raft all the way down to **Wet Willie's**.

## How to play
- Shoot the shots lit orange. They're **hazards** on the river, and each one moves your raft along.
- Hit the 2 green targets by **No Way Out** (the shot up the middle) to light a lock. Lock 3 balls there to start multiball.
- In multiball, shoot **Insanity Falls**, the steep ramp on the upper playfield, for the jackpot.
- Press the right flipper button all the way to use the small upper flipper.

## Tips
- **#1:** Make **No Way Out** your go-to shot. Multiball and lots of other awards run through it.
- Shoot **Insanity Falls** off the plunge for a 1 million point skill shot.
- If the **Whirlpool** offers **5X Playfield**, grab it. Most shots score 5x for 25 seconds.
- Hit the shots firmly. The ramps reward a strong flip.

## Fun facts
- Bigfoot is the only toy on the game. He's said to be modeled on the designer himself.
- A secret flipper code in attract mode shows a pair of dueling cows.
