💡 YOUR QUESTION ANSWERED: 180'–200' GOVEE "INDIVIDUAL LIGHTS" (PERMANENT OUTDOOR PUCKS) vs. CONTINUOUS LED STRIPS

The 180'–200' Govee kit with individual spaced lights (**Govee Permanent Outdoor Lights Pro**, puck lenses spaced every 16") is **NOT worse** than a continuous LED strip — it actually creates a **completely different, super cool architectural effect** depending on the zone:

1. WHERE THE 200FT INDIVIDUAL PUCKS WIN (POSTERS & TRUSSES)

Because each individual puck has a focused cone lens spaced every 16", running them along the 20ft upper loft front puts 1–2 dedicated mini spotlights on every single 18"×24" Billy Strings poster and casts dramatic scalloped V-cones of color across your wood roof trusses and under-loft joists!

2. WHERE SMOOTH STRIPS WIN (BAR LIP & PINBALL GLASS)

Directly above or behind the sloped pinball playfield glass and under the left bar counter lip, bare individual puck lenses can show up as bright point-reflections in the glass. A smooth continuous strip (or tucking the pucks behind the front wood header lip facing the joists) prevents any pinball glass glare.

3. THE ULTIMATE COMBO (SEE SERIES C BELOW)

Use your 180'–200' Govee Individual Puck Kit across the roof trusses, under the 10 Billy Strings posters, and tucked inside the under-loft perimeter lip — and pair it with a $44 Govee RGBIC strip behind the 6 pinball backboxes & under the cabinets!
